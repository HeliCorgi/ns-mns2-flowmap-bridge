## §2 — What exact NS gives `λ₂⁺` unconditionally (strain equation pushed; pressure Hessian unsigned) [D]

In the eigenframe, for simple eigenvalues (crossings are measure-zero for generic fields and harmless for the integral statements), first-order perturbation gives `e_i·(D_tS)e_i = D_tλ_i`, `e_i·(S²)e_i = λ_i²`, `e_i·(Ω²)e_i = −¼(|ω|² − ω_i²)` (`ω_i := ω·e_i`), and the viscous term `e_i·(ΔS)e_i = Δλ_i + 2Σ_{j≠i}(λ_j − λ_i)|e_i·∇e_j|²` (second-order perturbation; `|∇e_i|² = Σ_{j≠i}|e_j·∇e_i|²`). Hence the **exact eigenvalue equations**

> `D_tλ_i = −λ_i² + ¼(|ω|² − ω_i²) − p_{ii} + νΔλ_i + 2νΣ_{j≠i}(λ_j − λ_i)|e_i·∇e_j|²`, `p_{ii} := e_i·(∇²p)e_i`, `Σ_ip_{ii} = Δp = ½|ω|² − |S|²`.

What this gives, with **no sign assumed on `p_{22}`** (only the trace is known):
1. **Extremal eigenvalues have signed viscous frame terms**: for `λ₃` both `(λ_j − λ₃) ≤ 0` (viscosity depletes the largest eigenvalue beyond the Laplacian); for `λ₁` both `≥ 0`. **For `λ₂` the frame term `2ν[(λ₁−λ₂)|e₂·∇e₁|² + (λ₃−λ₂)|e₂·∇e₃|²]` is sign-indefinite** — the middle eigenvalue has no maximum-principle structure.
2. **The vortical source of `λ₂` is `¼|ω_{⊥2}|² ≥ 0`** (vorticity *perpendicular* to `e₂`); the DNS-generic alignment `ω ∥ e₂` makes it vanish. Together with the self-damping `−λ₂²` this is a Riccati-type balance `−λ₂² + ¼|ω_{⊥2}|²` whose fixed point is `λ₂ = ½|ω_{⊥2}|` — **not** smaller than `|S|`; no depletion of `λ₂⁺` relative to `|S|` follows.
3. **The pressure Hessian diagonal `p_{22}` carries no free integral**: `∫p_{22}dx = ∫e₂ᵀ(∇²p)e₂` is not a divergence (the frame varies), and the only free pressure identity on `ℝ³` is `∫Δp = 0` (i.e. `∫|S|² = ½∫|ω|²`). Miller's `⟨S,\mathrm{Hess}\,p⟩ = 0` removes the pressure from the *enstrophy* identity, not from the eigenvalue dynamics. With `ν = 0` and `p_{22} = 0` the eigenvalue system is the restricted-Euler (Vieillefosse) system, which blows up in finite time; **all regularising content sits in `p_{22}` and in the frame-viscous term, both unsigned for the middle eigenvalue.**
4. **Unconditional inventory for `λ₂⁺`:** (i) `λ₂⁺ ≤ |S|/√6` pointwise; (ii) `∫∫(λ₂⁺)² ≤ ‖u₀‖²₂/(24ν)` (energy); (iii) `∫ωᵀSω ≤ 2∫λ₂⁺|S|²` (the production is controlled by `λ₂⁺`-weighted strain energy — the mechanism of the criterion); (iv) the time-integrated production is signed-only: `∫₀^{T′}∫ωᵀSω = ½(E(T′)−E(0)) + ν∫‖∇ω‖²` (wall on the right). **Nothing critical; no free lower bound on the fraction of strain energy outside `λ₂⁺`.**

**The sub-wall margin.** HR-2 is strictly weaker than the `L⁴_tḢ¹` wall by the factor `X/E ≤ 1/12`, with equality only for axisymmetric-extension strain `(λ₁,λ₂,λ₃) ∝ (−2,1,1)` everywhere, and `X = 0` exactly when `λ₂ ≤ 0` (tube-forming strain `(−1,−1,2)`-type, or planar `(−1,0,1)`). **The head's content is therefore: at a blow-up, the strain energy carried by a positive middle eigenvalue must stay `L²_t`-bounded although `E` diverges — the strain at intense events must become tube-forming or planar in the `L⁴_tL²_x` sense.** The DNS phenomenology (Ashurst–Kerstein–Kerr–Gibson 1987 and successors: `⟨λ₁:λ₂:λ₃⟩ ≈ 3:1:−4`, positive skewness of `λ₂`, `ω ∥ e₂`) puts the generic fraction at `X/E ≈ 1/26` and *positive* at intense vorticity — **adverse**: generically `X ∝ E` with a fixed constant, so the head is the wall up to a constant unless intense events deplete `λ₂⁺`.
