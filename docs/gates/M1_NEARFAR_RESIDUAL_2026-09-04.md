## §1 — Audit of the GPT-side direct localized-Betchov estimate: [D] CORRECT, with one caveat; the shrinking-core judgment: CORRECT [D]

With `q + 4\det S = \mathrm{div}J_B`, `J_B = \tfrac43[(u·∇u)·∇u − ½u\,\mathrm{tr}A²]`, `|J_B| ≤ 2|u||∇u|²`, and a cutoff `χ` (`= 1` on `B_r`, supported in `B_{2r}`, `|∇χ| ≤ C/r`): `∫χq = −4∫χ\det S − ∫J_B·∇χ` (integration by parts, no boundary term for compact support) ✓. Then `|∫J_B·∇χ| ≤ (C/r)∫_{B_{2r}}|u||∇u|² ≤ (C/r)‖u‖₂‖∇u‖²₄` (Hölder `½+¼+¼`) ✓; `‖∇u‖₄ ≤ C‖∇u‖₂^{1/4}‖∇²u‖₂^{3/4}` (3-D Gagliardo–Nirenberg) ✓; hence `|∫J_B·∇χ| ≤ (C/r)‖u‖₂‖∇u‖₂^{1/2}‖∇²u‖₂^{3/2}`, and Young with exponents `(4/3, 4)` gives exactly

> `|∫J_B·∇χ| ≤ εν‖∇²u‖²₂ + C\,ε^{-3}ν^{-3}r^{-4}‖u‖₂⁴‖∇u‖₂²`, with `‖u(t)‖₂ ≤ ‖u₀‖₂` — **the boxed estimate is correct [D]** (constant `C = \tfrac14(\tfrac34)^3C_{GN}^6C_H^4`).

*Caveat (not an error):* the left side is localised to `B_{2r}`, the absorbing term `εν‖∇²u‖²₂` is the **global** dissipation; inside a *local* enstrophy budget only `ν∫χ|∇ω|²` is available, so a localised version must go through the cutoff commutator (Dascaliuc–Grujić's `∫|∇(φ^{1/2}ω)|² ≤ 2∫|∇ω|²φ + CR^{-2}∫|ω|²φ^{1/2}` [V]) and pays a further `R^{-2}` reservoir term. **Shrinking-core judgment [D]:** at `r = c\,s_v` the coefficient is `ν^{-3}r^{-4}‖u₀‖₂⁴ = c^{-4}ν^{-5}Λ²‖u₀‖₂⁴`, a Grönwall rate `∝ Λ²` on the enstrophy — worse than the BKM rate `Λ`; no closure follows, and since `∫χq = −4∫χ\det S + 4∫_{B_{2r}}\det(∇u)·(\text{cutoff shell})` the estimate only repackages the cubic invariant. The GPT audit's ROUTE-KILLED for "`J_B` as a transport mechanism" is **confirmed**; `β_B` stays parked (twenty-eighth record).

## §3 — Consequence before any numerics: fixed-scale filtered budgets are energy-trivial; the burden is scale uniformity [D]

At a fixed pair `(r, ℓ)` every term of the filtered budget is bounded by the energy: `O ≤ ∫|Ω_ℓ|² ≤ C_φ′ℓ^{-2}‖u₀‖²₂`, `P ≤ C_φ″ℓ^{-4}‖u₀‖²₂`, `|V_{near}| + |V_{far}| ≤ ‖S_ℓ‖_∞O ≤ Cℓ^{-7/2}‖u₀‖₂·ℓ^{-2}‖u₀‖²₂`, and the commutator forcing is bounded by `‖Ω_ℓ‖₂‖\mathrm{curl\,div}R_ℓ‖₂ ≤ Cℓ^{-1}‖u₀‖₂·ℓ^{-2}‖(u⊗u)‖₂-type` quantities — all finite for every Leray solution at fixed `ℓ`. Hence **no inequality between fixed-scale filtered quantities can constitute a burden reduction**; regularity information sits exclusively in the behaviour as `ℓ, r → 0` (or `r ↓ s_v` with `Λ → ∞`), where the free coefficients lose powers: near `σ^{-5}` (relative) but `M_{r,ρ}`-weighted, far `r^{-5/2}‖u₀‖₂` (absolute, from `‖S^{far}‖_∞ ≤ C(ρr)^{-3/2}‖Ω_ℓ‖₂ ≤ C(ρr)^{-3/2}ℓ^{-1}‖u₀‖₂` [D]), localisation `r^{-2}(1+M_r²)`, commutator none. The near-field absorption is therefore not where the wall was — the wall is the scale-uniform control of `V⁺_{far} + R_{comm}` (plus the reservoir coefficients), exactly as Yu concludes. This fixes what the numerics *can* decide: **which class carries the surplus and whether it has a sign** (structure), not whether a sub-wall inequality exists (it cannot at fixed scale).
