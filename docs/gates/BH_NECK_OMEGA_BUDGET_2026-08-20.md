## 2. The budget identity [D] (twice-derived; the pass's permanent kernel)

For `κ_R = ∫_R ω_θ dr dz = ∮_{∂R} u_pol·dl` (meridional measure `dA = dr dz`, no
`2πr`; `(z,r)` right-handed with out-of-plane `e_θ`):

> **Fixed region:**
> `dκ_R/dt = −∮_{∂R} ω_θ(u_pol·n)dl + ∮_{∂R}(Γ²/r³)dr + ν∮_{∂R}[∂_nω_θ + (ω_θ/r)n_r]dl`
> **Material region:** the advective flux vanishes **identically** (the transport is
> in exact 2-D conservation form: the vortex-stretching term `(u_r/r)ω_θ` *is* the
> 2-D compressibility defect `∇₂·u_pol = −u_r/r` and cancels — not "absorbed").

- Production density for `ω_θ` is `r^{−3}∂_z(Γ²)` (the `r^{−4}` of the seeded
  equation belongs to `ω_θ/r`); its exact contour form `∮(Γ²/r³)dr` telescopes,
  h-free and δ-free — (S-T) CONFIRMED unanimously, and sharpened to the
  **oscillation form** [D, critic]: since `∮f(r)dr = 0` on closed contours,
  `|∮(Γ²/r³)dr| ≤ ½·osc_R(Γ²)·(r₁^{−2} − r₂^{−2})` — production is driven by the
  *oscillation* of Γ, not its size (`osc ≤ Γ₀²` recovers the telescoped bound).
- **Enlargement lemma** [D]: the bound depends on the inner radius alone —
  enlarging R in z or outward in r is free; a δ_T-thin strip is dominated by the
  dyadic annulus `[r₁, 2r₁]`.
- **There is no viscous bulk sink** (R-NEG4): `Δω_θ − ω_θ/r² = ∂_r[(1/r)∂_r(rω_θ)] +

  claimed `−ν∫ω_θ/r²` bulk damping comes from a measure-bookkeeping error and does
  not exist. Viscosity enters the κ-budget **only** as an unsigned boundary flux
  (**T4** — unestimated by all four agents; the only viscous entry).
- **Independent confirmation (Kelvin/winding)** [D]: for a meridional material loop,
  `κ_pol(t) = κ₀ − ∮Γ∂_sθ ds` with `θ̇ = Γ/r²`; its time-derivative reproduces
