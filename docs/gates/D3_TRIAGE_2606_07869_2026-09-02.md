## §2. First-pass audit — load-bearing gaps found

**(G1) The strict-bridge no-saturator argument tests stationarity along an
inadmissible direction.** The contradiction (Prop 25.5 / Thm 32.3 / Thm D.9:
`0 = d/ds T_χ[Ĝ_s] = −(3/2)T_χ[G]`) uses the **space-only, L²(dµ₅)-normalized**
dilation `D_sG(Y,τ) = s^{5/2}G(sY, τ)` (§32.2, Lem 10.5). But the class on which
the maximizer is produced and Ekeland stationarity is asserted **requires
"solution-generated" endpoints** (Def 27.1; Def D.1(i)). The Navier–Stokes
scaling of `G` is, by the paper's own Lem 9.6, exponent **3** with time dilation
`λ²t`; the bridge dilation has exponent 5/2 and **frozen time**, so for `s ≠ 1`
the curve `D_sG` exits the solution-generated class. Stationarity of a
constrained maximizer holds only along curves that stay in the class; the
admissibility check (Thm 26.5) audits **only the nine ledger components** and
never addresses constraint (i). Two aggravating sub-gaps: **(G1b)** the
zero-ledger set `{L = 0}` is a constraint **boundary** (each `L_j ≥ 0` with
`L_j = 0` at the endpoint ⟹ `s = 1` minimizes `L_j` along any curve; one-sided
derivatives and second-order growth are generic), so first-order stationarity
gives a multiplier inequality, not `dT/ds = 0`; Def 26.1 *defines* the variation
admissible "in the sense that any nonzero one-sided derivative would select that
component" — assuming away exactly the issue. **(G1c)** the framework equivocates
between threshold-based "no active output" (Def 6.5, 15.1) and exact
componentwise zero (Def 4.2): sub-threshold cutoff/collar content makes the
"pure homogeneity" derivative (Lem 32.2, Cor A.8) drop genuinely nonzero
commutators — note `V_χ[D_sG] = s²V_χ[G]` is exact only for the **global** `V`,
not the χ-localized one. Without the strict bridge, alternative (C) is open and
Theorem 1.1 is unproved.

**(G3) The alternative-(C) class is not consistently defined at the point where
exactness matters.** Def 4.2 (zero-output) requires `R_RZ = 0`, where `R_RZ` is
"full-Dirichlet G-visibility … output"; Lem 35.5's exclusion of (C) requires the
same packet to "carry positive visibility" `V_χ[G] > 0` (indeed
`V_χ[G_n] = 1` in the master class, Def D.1(iv)). Under the natural reading the
(C)-class is empty and the bridge vacuous — with the visibility packets then
living in alternative (B), where the bridge is nonetheless invoked as a form
bound inside closed packets (Lem 14.6): vacuous-or-circular. The paper carries
**three different ledger definitions** at this point (Def 4.1/4.2; Def 25.1;
Def 34.1's `L_ext`, which silently drops `R_RZ` and adds a new component
`A_anc`).

**(G2) The routing half of the ledger is never quantitatively defined.**
`M_motion, R_proj, L_cas, M_macro` and the thresholds `τ_ledger` exist only as
verbal descriptions (Def 4.1, Def 15.1); obstructions are assigned to them **by
name** (Props 6.7–6.10, 8.4). Consequently the exhaustiveness of the four-way
classification (Prop 5.3), the routing-rank termination (Thm C.7 — a prose
reassignment argument), and the finiteness of alternative (B) (Lem 35.4) are not
checkable. In a proof by exhaustion, an alternative that is not precisely defined
is a hole, independent of (G1).

Softer flags (recorded, not load-bearing for the verdict): the bridge inequality
`|T| ≤ ϑV` is cubic-vs-quadratic in `G` and survives only via the `V = 1`
normalization + subthreshold absorption — legitimate where used, but it means the
"bridge" is a smallness statement, not a coercivity mechanism; the companion
reduction paper (§36) is absent; single author, no math affiliation; the
alternative-(D) collapse (§34) is an ε-regularity variant, which is classical in
strength and cannot by itself carry the theorem.

## §3. Adversarial verification of this audit

The gap-findings G1(a/b/c) and G2/G3 were handed, with verbatim excerpts, to two
independent adversarial verifiers instructed to **refute the auditor** (steelman
the paper). Outcomes:

**Verifier 2 (G2/G3 — ledger definitions):**

- **G2: GAP CONFIRMED, framing corrected.** Prop 5.3's four-way partition is
  *formally* exhaustive by excluded middle; the actual hole is that **class
  membership is undecidable** (four routing components and all thresholds lack
  quantitative definitions, so "some component above threshold" cannot be checked
  for any concrete packet) and that **the quantitative decrease behind Thm C.7 /
  Lem 35.4 does not exist in the paper**: "absorption after rebase" is not a rank
  decrease, so well-founded descent on the routing rank does not exclude
  infinitely many rank-constant absorption steps — exactly what "(B) cannot
  persist infinitely" must rule out; ruling it out needs a uniform quantum
  consumed per selection from a finite budget, and with thresholds lacking
  formulas no quantum exists. C.7's quoted proof moreover leans textually on the
  §34–35 exclusions, so the burden cannot be shuffled between branches. Repair
  requires **new mathematics** (explicit integral definitions of the four
  components, explicit thresholds, a monotone rank-plus-budget functional), not a
  reading choice. **This is a fatal branch on its own.**
- **G3: PARTIALLY REPAIRABLE — the strong form of §2's (G3) is withdrawn.** The
  charge "(C) is definitionally empty / Lem 35.5 vacuous-or-circular" is
  refutable by a consistent *capture-output reading*: `R_RZ` = the amount the RZ
  mechanism books into the ledger, not the raw functional `V_χ[G]`; Def 6.2's own
  sentence ("**This is not a finite ledger**") severs `V` from the ledger, and
  under that reading Lem 35.5 has the standard critical-element/Liouville shape.
  **Confirmed, however:** the paper runs **three inequivalent zero-output
  membership criteria** (Def 4.2 exact componentwise zero; Prop 5.3's proof
  "no component above threshold"; Lem 35.5 "first-threshold zero-output") at
  exactly the step where membership must be decidable, and every disambiguation
  leaves an unproven quantitative obligation (the subthreshold-to-exact-zero
  limiting-frame passage is asserted nowhere; the threshold reading needs a
  `c₀` vs `Στ_ledger` separation with no formulas for the thresholds). Separately
  confirmed: Def 34.1's new component `A_anc` is absent from Prop 5.3's
  partition, so "(D)-packet ⟹ `A_anc` = 0" is an undischarged premise (a
  zero-ledger, zero-transfer packet funded by backward-ancestor import is
  excluded by nothing printed).

**Verifier 1 (G1 — the variational core), with two corrections to §2's framing
adopted:**

- **G1a: GAP CONFIRMED — sharpened.** The verifier first *corrected* §2's
  emphasis: under the `V`-normalization the anomalous exponent of `T/V^{3/2}` is
  `s^{−3/2}` for **any** spatial-only dilation `s^aG(s·)` regardless of the
  amplitude exponent `a`, so the 5/2-vs-3 exponent discrepancy washes out; **the
  irreducible discrepancy with the paper's own Lemma 9.6 is the missing time
  dilation `λ²t`.** With that correction the gap is confirmed and strengthened:
  (i) preservation of the solution-generated constraint (Def D.1(i)) under `D_s`
  is nowhere proven, and the quoted admissibility machinery (Def 26.1 / Thm 26.5)
  by its own text checks only the ledger; (ii) the alternative reading that drops
  constraint (i) self-destructs — on that class the dilation identities are exact
  for interior-supported members, and running Ekeland-plus-dilation at
  near-maximizers would yield `sup|T| = 0`, i.e. `T ≡ 0` on all normalized
  zero-ledger `G`, which is false (`T` is a nondegenerate odd-in-`∂_z` cubic; the
  paper itself treats `T = 1` in-class as coherent); (iii) a recomposition of
  `D_s` from the genuine parabolic scaling (anomalous exponent `−1/2`) would need
  τ-invariance of endpoints, amplitude-projectivity of solution-generation, and
  scale-covariance of the ledger — none stated or proved. The `0 = −3/2`
  contradiction is unproven; the exclusion of alternative (C), hence Theorem 1.1,
  does not follow as written.
- **G1b: GAP CONFIRMED — refined in the paper's favor on one sub-point.** §2's
  "one-sided derivatives can be nonzero" is withdrawn: for smooth curves, both
  one-sided derivatives of a nonnegative `L_j` vanish at its zero (interior
  minimum in `s`), which is what Def 26.1's clause addresses. The confirmed
  failure is worse: collar content is generated at order `(s−1)²`, so the
  dilation curve **exits `{L = 0}` at second order for every `s ≠ 1`** — no
  in-class comparison points exist at any order — and on `{L_j = 0}` with
  `L_j ≥ 0` smooth, `∇L_j = 0`, so constraint qualification fails maximally
  (Fritz John degenerates to `0 = 0`): constrained maximality yields no
  `dT/ds = 0`. Def 26.1 is a vacuous tangency filter substituted for the
  membership condition Ekeland requires. Moreover the `s^{−3/2}` exponent pushes
  near-maximizers to collar-pinned configurations — the exit is generic exactly
  where stationarity is invoked.
- **G1c: GAP REFUTED** as an attack on the variational core: Def D.1(iii) grants
  exact componentwise zero at the endpoint, under which every first-order cutoff
  commutator provably vanishes (collar terms are at least quadratic in
  collar-localized data), and the threshold notions (Def 6.5/15.1) live in the
  selection machinery, not in Prop 25.5. Residual upstream burden noted (that
  real blow-up limits land in the exact-zero class rather than merely
  sub-threshold) — a different obligation, moot given G1a.
