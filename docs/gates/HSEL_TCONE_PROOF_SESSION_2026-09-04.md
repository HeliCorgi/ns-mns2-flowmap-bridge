## §3 — Incompressibility and the rank-3-filling core (checks 1–4) [D]

- **Check 3 — incompressibility does NOT freely block rank-3 filling.** `tr𝒫 = ∫ S:W\,dx` with `S` traceless (incompressibility, `trS = 0` pointwise) and `W = J^TJ ⪰ 0`; this is sign-indefinite and carries no lower bound on any principal direction of the *integrated* tensor. For `R₋` to be rank-3 at a time needs `𝒫 ≺ −νD` (all three eigenvalues negative); `eᵀ𝒫e = ∫(∂_e u)·S(∂_e u)` negative for all `e` means every channel vector `∂_e u` lies predominantly in the pointwise compressive strain cone — and while `trS = 0` forbids `S ≺ 0` pointwise, it does **not** forbid the three integrated quadratic forms `eᵀ𝒫e` from being simultaneously negative (different `e` weight different spatial regions). **No free obstruction; this is the open core.**
- **Check 4 — no free rank-3 block from geometry either.** Pressure is already exactly cancelled from the channel (proved, earlier session — `⟨∇∂_e p, ∂_e u⟩ = 0`), so it offers nothing here. Strain tracelessness is pointwise, not a constraint on `𝔑`. Vorticity alignment is DNS-generic *adverse* (R1 finding: gradient-like channel vectors align with the compressive eigenvector), i.e. it pushes toward filling, not against it. No free rank-3 prevention.
- **Checks 1–2 — the invariants and triple filling.** = §1: `det` is the triple-filling obstruction matching `λ_min`; pairwise overlap of per-time negative eigenspaces is precisely what makes `∫R₋` fill more directions than any single `R₋(t)` — the accumulation is superadditive in rank (rank-1-per-time can integrate to rank-3), so per-time thinness gives no free per-horizon rank defect.

- **Wide cone at each time** (`R₋(t)` rank 2–3 pointwise): `det` of the integrand is already positive per-time; `det𝔑` inherits wall-order growth if the negativity is wall-large — T-DET then needs an amplitude/anisotropy mechanism on `𝒫`'s negative spectrum (the production-anisotropy branch, SP-a-type).
