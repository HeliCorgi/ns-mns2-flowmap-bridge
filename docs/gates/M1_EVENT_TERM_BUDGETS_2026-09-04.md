## §2 — What each preregistered ratio can and cannot deliver (analytic assessment, independent of the numbers) [D]

The five ratios are exact decompositions of terms that appear in known identities; their *values* are what the runs measure, their *status as observables* is fixed by the following facts.

- **C1 `= P/G`, C2 `= P/P_{abs}`** (net stretching over gross, in two normalisations). `G = ∫|S||ω|²` and `P_{abs} = Σ_i∫|λ_i|ω_i²` have **no free companion** (`∫G\,dt` is of the order `∫E^{3/4}(‖∇ω‖²)^{3/4}` — wall-level). A uniform bound `C1 ≤ c < 1` changes the enstrophy inequality by a constant factor only (`½E′ ≤ cG − ν‖∇ω‖²`), i.e. it does not lower criticality. **Only a scaling trend** `C1 ≲ E^{-θ}` (or `Λ^{-θ}`), `θ > 0`, inside growth events would constitute sub-wall information (the "depletion exponent" question of Lu–Doering / Kang–Yun–Protas); such a trend is *not* covered by the preregistered "systematic ≤ 0.5" rule and, if seen, can only be reported as an observation for a future preregistration.
- **C3** (effective peak growth rate over stretching). The identity `\tfrac{d}{dt}\log Λ = α − ν|∇ξ|² + νΔ|ω|/|ω|` at `x_max` is exact (the maximum point has `∇|ω| = 0`); its time integral is `\log(Λ(T)/Λ(0))` — BKM's integrand split into stretching, twist dissipation (`≤ 0`) and core diffusion (`≤ 0` at a maximum). For a steady Burgers vortex the split is `α = −νΔ|ω|/|ω|`, twist `= 0`, i.e. C3 `= 0`. C3 therefore measures **which term cancels the stretching at the peak**; the corresponding criterion `∫‖(α − ν|∇ξ|² + νΔ|ω|/|ω|)₊‖_∞dt < ∞` *is* BKM (it is `\log Λ`'s growth) — no new observable, but a decomposition with decision value (it tells whether the twist term, the R3 refinement of the twenty-fourth record, is ever quantitatively relevant).
- **C4** (pressure-Hessian shielding of `λ₂` on the intense positive-`λ₂` set). The terms of `D_tλ₂ = −λ₂² + ¼|ω_{⊥2}|² − p_{22} + ν(ΔS)_{22}` are exact pointwise; their integrals over a *moving* set are not a conservation law, so C4 has **no free companion** and no bridge as it stands. Its value is **decision-relevant**: a systematically negative or small C4 means the pressure Hessian counteracts the vortical source of the middle eigenvalue in intense regions — the un-park trigger registered for HR-2 in the twenty-sixth record (a structural sign for `p_{22}` would be the missing lever of the `λ₂⁺` head); a C4 near 1 means the pressure does not shield.
- **C5 `= α_{far}/α`, C5′ `= α/|S|`** at `x_max`. The far strain admits *free* bounds through Constantin's `L¹` vorticity bound: `|S_{far}(x)| ≤ C‖ω‖_{L¹}r^{-3}`, so at the **`L¹`-sparseness radius** `r₁ = (K‖ω‖_{L¹}/Λ)^{1/3}` the far strain is `≤ Λ/K` — a free, sub-`Λ` bound on the strain induced from outside `r₁`; the near strain (inside `r₁`) is the self-induced strain of the intense structure, which vanishes for a straight tube and is `O(Λ·R/L_ξ)` for a tube of core radius `R` and axial coherence length `L_ξ`. **Bridge shape (conditional):** if at the maximum `α_{near} ≤ (1−c)α` with the near split at `r₁` and the far part free, then `\tfrac{d}{dt}\log Λ ≤ α_{far} + (1−c)α ≤ Λ/K + …` — a self-improving Grönwall on `\log Λ` with a *free* coefficient, i.e. genuinely sub-wall **if** `c` is uniform. The preregistered split is at the viscous scale `c(ν/Λ)^{1/2}`, not at `r₁`; the `r₁`-split is recorded here as the follow-up observable that a candidate would need (`r₁ ≫ (ν/Λ)^{1/2}` whenever `Λ ≫ ‖ω‖_{L¹}^{-2}ν^{3}`, i.e. always in the intense regime), and the viscous-scale values of C5 are its first, cruder, proxy.
- **Fat-core statistic.** Direct check of the twenty-fifth record's F-tube family in growth events: the minimal in-set run length through `x_max` in units of `(ν/Λ)^{1/2}` versus BFG's admissible half-length `(2c(M))^{-1}`; values `≫ 1` in every direction mean the BFG hypothesis fails at the maximum, as predicted for coherent cores with `Re_Γ ≳ 1`.

**Consequently the only routes from this study to a *sub-wall* observable are (i) a scaling trend of C1/C2 with intensity, or (ii) a uniform nonlocality fraction `c` in C5 with the split moved to the `L¹` radius; everything else is decision-relevant diagnostics.** This assessment was fixed before the results were read.

**Reading of the term budgets.** (i) The geometric depletion C1 is real, large and *systematic* in every run (net stretching `≤ 0.33` of the gross `∫|S||ω|²`, `≤ 0.11` for coherent tubes), but flat in intensity within events. (ii) The eigenframe sign cancellation C2 is partial and not systematic. (iii) At the maximum, the twist dissipation and the core diffusion cancel `25–70 %` of the stretching, with the core-diffusion term dominating (Burgers-like balance); the twist term carries `≈ 50 %` of the *global* enstrophy dissipation in all runs — the `−ν|ω||∇ξ|²` term of the `|ω|`-equation is not a small correction. (iv) The pressure Hessian shields the middle eigenvalue on the intense positive-`λ₂` set, increasingly with intensity, to complete cancellation in the tube run — **the structural sign registered as HR-2's un-park trigger (twenty-sixth record §6) is present at evidence grade in the intense regime, as a conditional statistic, not as a pointwise sign.** (v) The intense set is fat at `≥ 8–23` viscous lengths in every direction through the maximum in all runs — the F-tube prediction of the twenty-fifth record holds in every growth event computed (the viscous scale is `≈ 1` grid cell at 64³, so this is a resolved-scale statement about the structures, and a coarse one about the viscous scale itself).

## §5 — VERDICT: DIAGNOSTIC-ONLY

By the preregistered rule: **C1 is systematic** (`≤ 0.5` at every event output of every resolved run — E0, and of all diagnostic runs as well) but has **no free companion** and gives only a constant factor in the enstrophy inequality (§2); no other ratio is systematic (C2, C3 exceed 0.5; C4 exceeds 0.5 at event onsets in E0/E1 and is `≈ 0` only in the intense regime; C5 is degenerate at this resolution). Hence not OBSERVABLE-CANDIDATE; not NEGATIVE (systematic cancellations exist). **DIAGNOSTIC-ONLY.**

**Observations recorded for a future preregistration (not candidates, not claims):**
1. **Pressure-Hessian shielding of `λ₂` on the intense set, `C4 → 0` with intensity.** The natural statement to preregister next is a *conditional* inequality on the intense positive-`λ₂` set, e.g. `∫_{set}(−p_{22}) ≥ (1−κ)∫_{set}¼|ω_{⊥2}|² − ∫_{set}λ₂²` with `κ → 0` as `Λ` grows, at higher resolution (`96³–128³`, tail-resolved) and in more than one datum family; if robust, it is the first structural evidence for the missing sign in the `λ₂⁺` head — still with no bridge (the set moves; the twenty-sixth record §2.3), but it would justify an on-paper session on the conditional sign of `p_{22}` in intense regions.
2. **Nonlocality of the peak stretching and the `L¹`-radius route.** The near fraction is `≈ 0` at up to 8 viscous lengths; testing the bridge-shaped statement of §2 (free far-strain bound at `r₁`) requires a configuration with `‖ω‖_{L¹}/Λ ≪ (L/2)³`, i.e. an isolated intense structure in a large box (e.g. a vortex-ring pair in a `[0,2π)³` box at `128³` with `Λ ≳ 10²‖ω‖_{L¹}`) — a preregistrable follow-up.
3. **Twist share `≈ ½` of the enstrophy dissipation** — the `−ν|ω||∇ξ|²` term is quantitatively co-dominant; any future use of the `|ω|`-equation (R3 refinement, the dual `L¹` identity R1) should not neglect it.
4. **Resolution:** the strict `10⁻⁵` tail rule at 64³ leaves only the mild TG event resolved; E1/E3/E4 sit at `4–6×10⁻⁵`. All qualitative conclusions are uniform across the five runs; the quantitative ones should be re-established at 96³–128³ before any promotion.

**Consequences (recommendations; user acts):** (1) no head is promoted; the numerical lane stays open with the two follow-ups above as the recorded next preregistrations (both cheap; (1) is the more decision-relevant since it feeds the only registered un-park trigger); (2) the Hou finite-cylinder closure remains unstarted and unnecessary for these objectives; (3) the E2b (96³) result is appended in §7 when available and can only strengthen or weaken the tube-run row, not the verdict.
