## §3 — Named proof-route constraints on H-SEL (new, recorded for any future proof commission)

- **C-1 (dynamical-only) [D from V-adj/V-num].** Lu–Doering-type instantaneous maximizers satisfy `∫ω·Sω ~ ℰ³/ν³` at enstrophy `ℰ`, forcing `‖S‖_∞ ≳ ℰ²/ν³`, i.e. `‖∇u‖_∞ ≳ ‖∇U‖₂⁴/ν³` on explicit divergence-free fields of arbitrary enstrophy. Hence **every member `p < 4` is false as a kinematic inequality over divergence-free fields**: any proof of H-SEL must show the *flow* avoids such configurations beyond datum-dependent constants — it must consume the evolution, not just pointwise-in-time functional analysis.
- **C-2 (subcritical datum-dependence essential) [V].** Palasek (arXiv:2509.18595) rules out a priori sup-bounds whose constants factor through **critical** norms of the datum, in the well-posed smooth setting. Any proof of H-SEL in which `Q₀` degrades to critical-norm dependence is false; the `‖u0‖_{H³}` (subcritical) dependence is essential and cannot be weakened to `BMO^{−1}`/`B^{−1}_{∞,∞}`.
- **C-3 (exact-NS structure; FC-086 located at rung level) [V + D].** Tao's averaged model preserves the energy identity and every `W^{s,p}` bilinear estimate, and its blow-up cascade (bounded energy, geometric frequency concentration) **violates the head-analogue for every `p < 5/2`** (`‖∇u‖_∞ ~ N^{5/2}e^{1/2}` vs `‖∇U‖₂^p ~ (Ne^{1/2})^p`; at `p = 2` the violation rate is `N^{1/2}` — [D] from the paper's stated structure, confirming and refining the head-reduction audit's §9.3 row). So a proof of any member `p < 5/2` cannot go through energy structure + harmonic-analysis bilinear estimates alone. Members `p ∈ [5/2, 4]` are **not** violated by the cascade — the FC-086 pressure applies specifically to the operative low-`p` range.
