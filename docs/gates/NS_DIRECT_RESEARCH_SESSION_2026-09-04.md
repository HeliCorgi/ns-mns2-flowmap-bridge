## §1 — Routes explored (each carried to a definite formula-level conclusion)

**R1 — the exact dual-weighted `L¹` vorticity identity and a local `L¹` mass estimate [D, proved; FAILCASE by dimension].**
From `D_t|ω| − νΔ|ω| = (α − ν|∇ξ|²)|ω|` on `{ω ≠ 0}` (regularised by `φ_ε = (|ω|²+ε²)^{1/2} − ε` at zeros; the regularised equation is `D_tφ_ε − νΔφ_ε ≤ α₊φ_ε + ε|S|`, the `ε|S|` term is `o(1)` after integration against a bounded test weight) and any non-negative solution `w` of the **backward** transport–diffusion equation `−∂_tw = u·∇w + νΔw`, `w(·,T) = ψ ≥ 0` (mass-conserving and `w ≤ ‖ψ‖_∞` because `div u = 0`), the duality pairing gives the exact identity
> `∫|ω(T)|ψ\,dx = ∫|ω₀|\,w(0)\,dx + ∫₀^T∫(α − ν|∇ξ|²)|ω|\,w\,dx\,dt`  (transport and diffusion cancel exactly).
Nash's inequality (`‖f‖₂^{10/3} ≤ C‖∇f‖₂²‖f‖₁^{4/3}` in 3-D) applied to `s ↦ ‖w(T−s)‖₂²`, whose derivative is `−2ν‖∇w‖₂²` for *any* divergence-free drift, gives the drift-independent bound `‖w(T−s)‖_∞ ≤ min(‖ψ‖_∞,\; C‖ψ‖₁(νs)^{-3/2})`. With `ψ = 1_{B_r(x₀)}` and `P(t) := ∫α₊|ω|\,dx ≤ ‖∇u(t)‖₂²/√2`:
> `∫_{B_r(x₀)}|ω(T)| ≤ C\,r³(νT)^{-3/2}‖ω₀‖_{L¹} + ∫_{T−r²/ν}^{T}P\,dt + C\sum_{k≥0}2^{-3k/2}∫_{T−2^{k+1}r²/ν}^{T}P\,dt`, uniformly in `x₀`.
Assessment: every term has dimension `L`; the estimate says the local `L¹` vorticity mass is controlled by the *recent* enstrophy production and cannot concentrate as `T ↑ T*` (uniform modulus `m(τ) = ∫_{T*−τ}^{T*}P → 0`). It is **dominated by trivial bounds in the singular regime**: on the Harnack ball `B(x_max, c‖ω‖_∞^{-1/2})` it yields only the time-averaged `⟨E⟩_{window} ≳ ν‖ω‖_∞^{1/2}`, whereas `E(s) ≥ ‖ω(s)‖_∞²·|B| /4 ≳ ‖ω(s)‖_∞^{1/2}` holds instantaneously; combined with a Morrey-type `ε`-regularity it reproduces a hypothesis strictly stronger than CKN's local one. Chain: `exact NS identity ⟶ new (elementary) estimate ⟶ no continuation mechanism reached` (the estimate is one length-dimension above every known criterion).

**R2 — a scale-invariant weighted `L¹` identity with a pointwise viscous sink [D, proved; FAILCASE].** With the critical weight `φ = |x−x₀|^{-1}` (`Δφ = −4πδ_{x₀}`) and `x₀ = x₀(t)` a fluid trajectory:
> `\frac{d}{dt}∫\frac{|ω|}{|x−x₀(t)|}dx = ∫\frac{α|ω|}{|x−x₀|} + ∫|ω|\,(u(x)−u(x₀))·∇φ − ν∫\frac{|ω||∇ξ|²}{|x−x₀|} − 4πν\,|ω(x₀(t),t)|`.
All four terms are critical (dimension `L^{-2}` per unit time); the last is a *pointwise* sink equal to `4πν` times the vorticity at the trajectory point (huge at a singularity), the twist term is a second sink. Assessment: the two sources are bounded only by `(‖S‖_∞ + ‖∇u‖_∞)·∫|ω|/|x−x₀|` — a BKM-rate Grönwall; and even a bound on the functional `sup_{x₀}∫|ω|/|x−x₀|` (the critical Morrey class `∫_{B_r}|ω| ≲ r`) is not a known continuation mechanism without smallness. Chain: `identity ⟶ no estimate`. Recorded as a clean critical identity; nothing more.

**R3 — the effective stretching rate at the maximum [D; trivial extension, FAILCASE].** The `|ω|` equation with its twist term gives, at the maximum point, `\frac{d}{dt}‖ω‖_∞ ≤ (α − ν|∇ξ|²)(x_{max})\,‖ω‖_∞`, hence the criterion `∫₀^T‖(α − ν|∇ξ|²)₊(t)‖_∞dt < ∞ ⟹` no blow-up (BKM). This weakens the known `∫‖α₊‖_∞dt < ∞` criterion by the twist dissipation; NS supplies `α` and `|∇ξ|²` only in `|ω|`-weighted `L¹` form (`∫∫α|ω|` and `ν∫∫|ω||∇ξ|²` are both free and both dimension `L`), never in sup form. Not new information.

**R4 — the critical `L^{3/2}` vorticity identity and its kinematic non-closure [D; FAILCASE with explicit obstruction].** `\frac{d}{dt}∫|ω|^{3/2} = \tfrac32∫α|ω|^{3/2} − \tfrac34ν∫|ω|^{-1/2}|∇|ω||² − \tfrac32ν∫|ω|^{3/2}|∇ξ|²`, all three terms critical; the two dissipations sum to `\tfrac34ν∫|ω|^{-1/2}|∇ω|²` (up to the factor 2 on the twist part). A closure `∫α|ω|^{3/2} ≤ C∫|∇ω|²|ω|^{-1/2}` **is false for divergence-free fields**: for a vorticity blob of size `R` and amplitude `Ω` the ratio LHS/RHS `∼ ΩR²` (the circulation Reynolds number), unbounded; consistently, the only closure is the classical small-critical-norm one (`‖ω(t)‖_{3/2} ≤ cν`, obtained by Hölder `∫|S||ω|^{3/2} ≤ C‖ω‖_{3/2}‖ω‖_{9/2}^{3/2}` and Sobolev on `|ω|^{3/4}`). If closed, the mechanism would be genuine: `ω ∈ L^∞_tL^{3/2}_x ⟹ u ∈ L^∞_tL³_x ⟹` regular (Escauriaza–Seregin–Šverák 2003, Leray–Hopf class on `ℝ³`; quantitative form Tao 2019). Chain: `identity ⟶ [kinematically false inequality; dynamics required] ⟶ (mechanism exists)`.

**R5 — `L^p` vorticity via Constantin's `L¹` bound [D; recovers known walls].** Interpolating `‖ω‖_r ≤ ‖ω‖₁^{2/r−1}‖ω‖₂^{2−2/r}` with the free `‖ω‖₁ ≤ C₀` gives `ω ∈ L³_tL^{3/2}_x` unconditionally (`∫‖ω‖_{3/2}³dt ≤ C₀E₀/(2ν)`), but this quantity has dimension `L²` — *worse* than the energy; in the `L^p` identity the stretching is absorbed either by smallness of `‖ω‖_{3/2}` (Hölder with `r = 3/2`) or by `∫‖∇u‖₂⁴dt` (Hölder with `r = 2`) — the `L⁴_tḢ¹` wall. Nothing between.

**R6 — Constantin's kernel through the twist dissipation [D; conditional only].** `|α(x)| ≤ c∫₀¹∫|∇ξ(x+sy)||ω(x+y)||y|^{-2}dy\,ds`; inserting `|∇ξ| = |ω|^{-1/2}(|ω|^{1/2}|∇ξ|)` with `|ω|^{1/2}|∇ξ| ∈ L²_{x,t}` (free) leaves a negative power of `|ω|` at the intermediate point — usable only under a *magnitude-coherence* hypothesis along segments (conditional; the same shape as the HR-1 gap (G2)). The vortex-line identity `∫α|ω|² = −∫u_∥∂_s(|ω|²/2) − ∫|ω|²κ\,(u·n)` (`∂_s` = derivative along vortex lines, `κ` curvature, `n` normal) was re-derived for the geometric route; it is classical and carries no free bound.
